# ⚔️ Wealth-Cultivation

A fantasy-themed personal finance and net-worth tracker inspired by Xianxia cultivation novels. Monitor your financial Qi, track realm breakthroughs, and ascend through 12 stages of prosperity.

## 📌 Overview

Wealth-Cultivation gamifies personal finance by mapping real-world savings and net worth onto traditional cultivation realms. Instead of dry spreadsheets, every milestone becomes a realm breakthrough—turning disciplined saving, smart investing, and asset protection into an epic journey toward financial immortality.
