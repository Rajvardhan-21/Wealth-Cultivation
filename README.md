# Wealth-Cultivation
A fantasy-based cultivation tracker to monitor power, track realm progression, and achieve new breakthroughs.
 
### Cultivation Levels (Wealth Points)

1. Qi Gathering – 1 to 999 points. The starting stage of wealth cultivation, where you begin your financial journey by developing saving habits and building your first wealth.

2. Foundation Building – 1,000 to 9,999 points. Establishing a strong financial foundation through regular savings, smart decisions, and consistent wealth accumulation.

3. Golden Core – 10,000 to 99,999 points. A major breakthrough where your financial strength becomes more stable and your wealth starts growing significantly.

4. Nascent Soul – 1,00,000 to 9,99,999 points. Reaching an advanced stage of financial growth, showing discipline, patience, and the ability to manage greater wealth.

5. Soul Formation – 10,00,000 to 99,99,999 points. Strengthening your financial power and developing the ability to maintain and expand your wealth.

6. Void Amalgamation – 1,00,00,000 to 9,99,99,999 points. Entering a higher realm of prosperity, where financial growth and stability reach an impressive level.

7. Body Integration – 10,00,00,000 to 99,99,99,999 points. Achieving exceptional financial stability and building a strong foundation for long-term prosperity.

8. Transcend Tribulation – 1,00,00,00,000 to 9,99,99,99,999 points. Overcoming major financial challenges and proving your ability to protect and grow your accumulated wealth.

9. Mahayana – 10,00,00,00,000 to 99,99,99,99,999 points. Approaching the peak of wealth cultivation, where financial strength and success reach extraordinary heights.

10. Loose / Human Immortal – 1,00,00,00,00,000 to 9,99,99,99,99,999 points. Crossing beyond ordinary financial limits and entering the path towards immortal-level prosperity.

11. Earth Immortal – 10,00,00,00,00,000 to 99,99,99,99,99,999 points. Reaching an elite level of wealth, representing independence, lasting stability, and extraordinary financial power.

12. Heavenly Immortal – 1,00,00,00,00,00,000+ points. The supreme stage of wealth cultivation, representing the ultimate achievement of financial growth, prosperity, and success.

Ultimate Goal: Ascend from Qi Gathering to Heavenly Immortal and achieve supreme wealth cultivation!
